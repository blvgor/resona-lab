About the simulation

Why does the sand draw?

When the plate vibrates, some areas move a lot and others stay almost still. Sand collects along these nodal lines, and that is how Chladni figures appear.

Frequencies and mode shapes are calculated for a thin rectangular plate supported on all four edges. In Patterns mode two modes are mixed. On a square plate their frequencies are equal; on a rectangular plate their ratio changes.

In Manual mode the app calculates the response to a point source: 16 of 81 modes with the biggest contribution are used. Material, thickness and size change the frequencies. Field shows the vibration, Contours show lines of equal displacement.

Grain movement is approximate: particles move towards areas with lower amplitude. It is a tool for exploring and making graphics, not an engineering model of a real setup.
